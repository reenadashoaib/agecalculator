About this age calculator

This site does one job: it tells you exactly how old you are, or how old you'll be on any date you choose. It shows your age in years, months and days, the total number of weeks, days, hours and minutes you've been alive, and how long until your next birthday.

Who it's for

People use it to fill in forms that ask for an exact age, to check eligibility dates for school, retirement or a driving licence, to plan milestone birthdays, or just out of curiosity about how many days they've been around.

How it works

Everything is calculated in your browser. Your date of birth isn't sent to our server or saved anywhere. The method counts full years first, then full months, then the remaining days, which matches how ages are written on official documents.
