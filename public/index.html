How your age is worked out

The calculator counts the full years since your date of birth, then the full months since your last birthday, then the days left over. It's the same method you'd use on a passport or insurance form, so a person born on 14 May 1990 is 34 years, 0 months and 0 days old on 14 May 2024.

Months don't all have the same length, so the leftover days are counted from the same date in the previous month. If that date doesn't exist (say you were born on the 31st and the month only has 30 days), the last day of that month is used instead.

Questions people ask

What if I was born on 29 February?

In years without a 29 February, this calculator treats 28 February as your birthday. Some countries use 1 March for legal purposes, so check locally if it matters for a document.

Why does the total in weeks look smaller than I expected?

Weeks only count full seven-day blocks. The days that don't make up a whole week are left out of that number but still appear in the total days.

Can I find out how old someone will be on a future date?

Yes. Change the "Age on" date to any day in the future or the past and press Calculate age.

Is my date of birth stored?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and your date of birth is never sent to our server. If you copy a link to your result, the date is part of that link, so only share it with people you trust.

Does the time zone change the result?

Years, months and days use calendar dates only, so time zones don't affect them. The live seconds count assumes you were born at midnight in your current time zone.
