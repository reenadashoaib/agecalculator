Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you use this website and how it's used.

Your date of birth

The age calculator runs entirely in your browser. The dates you enter are not sent to our server and are not stored. If you use "Copy link to this result", the dates are included in that link, so anyone you share it with can see them.

Advertising

This site shows ads from Google AdSense to pay for running it. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to show ads and to measure how they perform.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website and other websites.
  • Google's advertising cookies let it and its partners show you ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can turn off personalised advertising in Google's Ad Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services for more detail.

Consent

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you'll be asked whether you agree to cookies for personalised ads before they're used. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy link in that message.

Hosting and logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Your browser storage

The site saves a small flag in your browser's session storage so that a full-screen ad or a closed video ad isn't shown again during the same visit. It's cleared when you close the browser tab.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change too. Questions about privacy can be sent to hello@example.com.
